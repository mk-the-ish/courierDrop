The "Handshake" Icon: A custom icon showing two hands connecting through a digital shield. Use this in the UI when the "Three-Way Handshake" is active.

The "Corridor" Map Style: Use a custom Mapbox or Google Maps style that de-saturates the city (greyscale) and highlights your Active Corridors in "Transit Teal."

Color               Hex                   CodePsychology
"Transit Teal"      #008080             Represents movement, efficiency, and a modern tech feel.
"Safe Slate"        #2F4F4FA              deep, dark grey/green that provides contrast and feels "grounded" and secure.
"Alert Amber"       #FFBF00             Used sparingly for "Virtual Stop" markers and notifications (represents caution/readiness).
"Cloud White"       #F8F9FA             For clean UI backgrounds to keep the "Offline-First" app looking lightweight.
